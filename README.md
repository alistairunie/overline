# Overline

Live tracking for your player props, multis and slips. It's free and runs in your browser, so there's nothing to install.

**[Open Overline](https://overlinestats.com/)**

Overline was called ClutchUp up to version 4.10. Your picks, slips and settings carry over.

Overline moved to overlinestats.com in version 5.2. The old github.io address sends you here and brings your picks, slips, history and settings with you.

## What it does

- **Keeps your picks in your account.** Sign in with your email (no password, just a code we email you) and your picks, slips, results, ladder and most settings are saved to your account, so they're on every phone and browser you sign in on. Your Discord webhook and price keys stay on your device. See [Accounts and privacy](https://overlinestats.com/privacy.html).
- **Tracks props live.** Pick a player, a stat and your line, then watch it count up as the game goes. You can see how far off the line they are, the pace they're on and their chance of getting there.
- **Sends alerts.** You get a notification when a pick lands or busts, or when a multi is one leg away. Once a pick is over its line it goes quiet. Quiet hours let you sleep through the overnight games: only results notify, and everything else waits on the Alerts tab.
- **Sub on, play on.** If your soccer player comes off, the bet moves to the player who replaced him and both their numbers count, the same as bet365's Sub On Play On. It moves once, and only for bets on More. You can turn it off in Settings or on any pick.
- **Keeps your prices.** Add or change the price on any pick you're tracking, and on each leg of a slip. With the legs priced, a multi's price is the legs multiplied, and a void leg comes out of it for you. A same game multi keeps the price you typed, the way bookies price them.
- **Follows your slips.** Group your legs into the slip you actually placed. It tracks every leg, ticks each one off as it goes over its line, settles the slip when the games finish and keeps your profit and hit rate on the Results tab. Picks and slips are listed by start time, so whatever goes first is at the top.
- **Shows what a slip's worth.** Once a game starts, each open slip shows its chance of landing and what it's worth right now, so you can tell if a cash out offer is any good. Tap a player on a slip or a pick to open their game.
- **Handles void legs.** If your bookie voids a leg, tap **Edit** on the slip and void it there. The leg comes out of the slip and you can type in the new price your bookie gives you. When the app voids a leg itself (a player who didn't play, say), a multi's price drops by that leg's price straight away. A same game multi gets repriced by the bookie, so the app asks you to type in the new price before it settles.
- **Team news.** Before a game, you get a heads up if a player in your picks is ruled out or questionable, and in soccer if they're on the bench once the line-up is out.
- **Team lists before the start.** Until the line-ups are out, each team is grouped by what its players did last game: who started, who came off the bench and who didn't play. Anyone ruled out is listed at the bottom, along with soccer players sent off in their last game in that competition. Soccer counts cup and European games, so the last game really is the last one.
- **Adds bets from a screenshot.** Screenshot your bet slip and Overline reads it and adds the legs. It knows the layouts of Sportsbet, Bet365, Neds, Ladbrokes, Dabble, Betr and TAB slips, in light or dark mode. The screenshot is read on your phone and never uploaded anywhere.
- **Reads every game.** Open a game and the Tips view gives a predicted score, each side's chance, the calls on the main markets, the best bets, the best matchups and a same game multi. It weighs the prices, recent form, rest, who's out and, for outdoor games, the weather. On the team lists, a gold or green tag marks each player's best matchup.
- **Player cards.** Tap a player and you get his card: how far his projection sits over your line, the matchup grade against tonight's side (the card's colour), where he ranks at his position and his last 10. Under it is the graph, each game a bar against the line with his minutes across the top, and you can move the line, switch between the last 5, 10, 20, the season or tonight's opponent, and see his last five for every main stat.
- **A picture for every player.** NBA, WNBA, NFL, NHL and tennis players have ESPN's headshots. For the NBL, AFL, NRL and soccer it's the photo from the player's Wikipedia page, credited on his card, and where there isn't one, his team's jersey with his number.
- **Shows who's on.** During a game your basketball picks say whether the player's on the court or on the bench, and soccer picks whether he's on the pitch. The game's player list marks whoever's on with a green dot.
- **Flags streaks.** A flame and a number mark a player who's landed his line four or more games in a row, on tips and on your picks.
- **Shows a player's hit rates.** When you pick a line, the player's games show as bars against it, each with the number and who it was against. Above them is how often he's cleared it in the last 5, the last 10, the whole season and against tonight's opponent, last season's games included. Below is his matchup grade for that stat and what he projects to.
- **Finds plays.** The Plays tab reads every sport, not just the leagues on your Games tab (you can switch it to just yours), and has eight tools:
  - **Tips** lists the best bets across every game in the next day and a half, with the chance of each and the best price found. Tap one to see the rest of that game.
  - **Matchups** shows what each team gives up to each position, from the last few weeks of box scores: rebounds to centres, passing yards to quarterbacks, shots to defencemen, shots and fouls to midfielders and so on. It pairs that with where the player ranks at his position, so a team that lets centres rebound the most against one of the best rebounding centres is graded elite and gets a gold border. The rest are graded great, good, even or tough on a colour scale. Each spot comes with a projection, the player's average moved by how much that defence gives up, set against the bookie's line where there is one: Jokić projects 13.9 rebounds against a line of 11.5. **Every team** ranks all the defences for one position and stat. **Today's elite** lists the day's elite spots across every sport and ticks them off as they land, with a recap of the day before: what landed, what missed and the overall. **Record** tracks how elite spots go over time: all time, the last 30 and 7 days, week by week, by league, by stat and against the line or the player's average. Works for the NBA, WNBA, NBL, NFL, NHL and the big soccer leagues (EPL, A-League, LaLiga, Serie A, Bundesliga and Ligue 1). In soccer a player's own numbers come from his starts.
  - **Multi** builds a multi to the price you want, one leg per game, picked for the best chance of landing.
  - **Check** rates a bet before you place it. Upload a screenshot of your slip, or pick one you're tracking, and it breaks down each leg: the chance it lands, its fair price, the best price around, whether your price is good value and the matchup grade with its projection. Type in or fix any leg's price and its value updates straight away. Then it does the same for the whole bet, points out the weakest leg and suggests a better one from the same game.
  - **Ladder** finds the next step for your ladder, a single or double in your price range with the best chance of landing.
  - **Screener** lists every prop on the next day's games, filtered by hit rate, price and average.
  - **Pick'em** builds More/Less entries for pick'em apps, one player per game.
  - **100%** shows the props that landed in every one of a player's last 5, 10 or 15 games, team by team.
- **Keeps score on its tips.** Every tip is saved before its game and marked after, so you can see how often they land and whether the chances hold up.
- **Covers lots of sports.** NBA, WNBA, NBL, NFL, NHL, AFL, NRL, soccer (EPL, A-League, Champions League and plenty more) and tennis (ATP and WTA singles).
- **Compares prices.** DraftKings and Bet365 prices come through ESPN. Add your own free key from [The Odds API](https://the-odds-api.com) to compare Sportsbet, TAB, Ladbrokes, Neds and the other Aussie bookies, or from [PropLine](https://prop-line.com) for Pinnacle. Each tip then shows the best price and which bookie has it. Pick'em apps like PrizePicks pay a flat amount rather than real odds, so their numbers are left out.
- **Posts its best bets to Discord.** Add a webhook and it posts the day's best bets each morning, along with the elite matchups, then edits the post with a tick or a cross as each one lands. When they're all done it posts a recap with the day's record and the week's. You can also post a multi, a pick'em entry or a screener prop it found. Your own picks and slips stay off the channel.
- **Helps you stick to a budget.** Set a weekly limit and the Slips tab shows what's left, with a heads up before a slip goes past it.

## How to use it

1. Open the link on your phone.
2. Put it on your home screen so it opens like an app.
   - **iPhone:** in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
   - **Android:** open the browser menu, then tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
3. Go to **Games**, tap a game, then tap a player (or **Match** for game bets). Pick the stat, your line and More or Less, then add it.
4. In **Settings**, turn on notifications and send yourself a test alert. On iPhone this only works once it's on your home screen.
5. Leave it open during games.

A few more things to try:

- **Placed a multi?** On **My picks** tap **Make a slip**, tick the legs, then put in your stake and price.
- **Got a screenshot of your slip?** Tap **Add from screenshot** and choose it. Check the legs it found, then add them.
- **Not sure about a bet?** Go to **Plays**, then **Check**, and upload the slip before you place it. You can also tap **Check** on any slip you're tracking.
- **Want to see it working first?** Go to **Settings** and tap **Start demo game**. It plays a fake basketball game in fast motion and sends real alerts.

## Good to know

- **Keep the page open.** The web version only tracks while it's open, in any tab. Once you close it, nothing updates, no alerts come through and the morning Discord post waits until you're back. If you're on Android, you can get the app from **Settings**. It keeps tracking with your phone locked and posts to Discord in the background.
- **Your picks stay on your device.** There are no accounts. Everything is saved in your browser, so it won't carry over to another phone, and clearing your browser data wipes it.
- **Use one tab at a time.** If it's open in two tabs, the second one waits until you close the first.
- **Stats can lag.** Live stats come from ESPN and can be up to a minute behind the broadcast. Bookies settle on official stats, which sometimes differ.
- **Some picks settle differently:**
  - Soccer settles on 90 minutes, so extra time doesn't count. With sub on, play on, a bet that moved to a sub settles on both players' totals.
  - NFL yardage can go backwards, so those picks only settle at full time.
  - In tennis, if a player retires, match winner bets stand once a set is finished. Other tennis bets stand only if they'd already been decided, otherwise they're void. A walkover voids everything.
- **It's a tracker, not a bookie.** It never places bets or touches your betting accounts.

## Privacy

There are no accounts, ads or analytics. Overline only connects to ESPN's public scores feeds, ESPN's image server for logos and headshots, Open-Meteo for the weather at outdoor games, Discord if you add a webhook, and The Odds API or PropLine if you add a key. Your keys stay on your device.
