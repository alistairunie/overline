Accounts and privacy

Overline is free, and you don't need an account to use it. An account keeps your picks safe and lets you use them on more than one phone or browser.

What your account keeps

  • Your email address, so we can send you a code when you sign in.
  • Your saved picks, slips, results and ladder.
  • Your Overline settings, apart from your Discord webhook and any price service keys. Those stay on your device.
  • When it was last saved, and whether that was from the Android app or the website.

Who can see it

Only you. Nothing in your account is public unless you choose to share it.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your details, and we don't send marketing emails. The only emails you'll get from Overline are sign-in codes you asked for.

Where it's kept

Accounts are stored with Supabase, the database service Overline uses. Sign-in codes are emailed through Resend.

Deleting your account

In Overline, go to Settings and tap Delete my account. Your account and everything saved in it are deleted straight away. The picks already on your device stay there until you remove them.

Age

Overline is for people 18 and over.
